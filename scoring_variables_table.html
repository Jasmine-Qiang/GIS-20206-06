# Variable Raw column Direction Source Notes
1 s_cafeCafe & boba access cnt_cafe ↑ higher OSM Count of cafes and tea/boba shops within tract. Tags: amenity=cafe, shop=tea. Boba merged into this category (no separate boba score).
2 s_gymGym access cnt_gym ↑ higher OSM Count of fitness centres within tract. Tag: leisure=fitness_centre.
3 s_groceryGrocery access cnt_grocery ↑ higher OSM Count of supermarkets and grocery stores. Tags: shop=supermarket, shop=grocery.
4 s_parkPark access cnt_park ↑ higher OSM Count of parks within tract. Tag: leisure=park.
5 s_transitTransit access cnt_transit ↑ higher OSM Combined count of bus stops (highway=bus_stop) and rail stations (railway=station). Roadmap's separate bus_stop and train_station are merged here.
6 s_restaurantRestaurant access cnt_restaurant ↑ higher Business Tax Count of restaurants from SD Business Tax dataset (teammate's data). Not in original roadmap — added as scope extension.
7 s_beachBeach access cnt_beach ↑ higher Business Tax Count of beach access points and lifeguard stations (teammate's data). ⚠ pending — currently all zeros
8 s_walkWalkability walk_index ↑ higher EPA EPA National Walkability Index (2021), scale 1–20. Block groups aggregated to tract level by mean. Missing tracts filled via parent/grandparent GEOID imputation.
9 s_coastCoastline proximity coast_dist_m ↓ lower TIGER Euclidean distance (meters) from tract centroid to nearest point on California coastline (TIGER/Line 2023). Inverted at normalization: closer = higher score.
10 s_downtownDowntown proximity downtown_dist_m ↓ lower Computed Euclidean distance (meters) from tract centroid to downtown SD (Civic Center, −117.1611, 32.7157). Inverted at normalization: closer = higher score.
11 s_rentRent affordability rent_zori ↓ lower Zillow ZORI ZORI rent index, April 2026, joined via ZCTA polygon spatial join. 25 missing tracts imputed with county median. Inverted at normalization: lower rent = higher score.
